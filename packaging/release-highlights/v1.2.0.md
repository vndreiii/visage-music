- **Queue:** right-click an upcoming track for Play now, Play next, Move up/down, Move to bottom and Remove; drag and drop (grab the handle, or long-press the row) reorders the queue.
- **Lyrics:** a floating **− / +** pill adjusts the lyrics delay per song (100 ms steps, tap the value to reset). Sync no longer runs ahead when playback stalls, and LRC `[offset:]` tags are honoured.
- **Lyrics cache:** fetched lyrics are remembered per song together with their delay, so they aren't fetched again. If you switch lyrics provider for a song, the one you last chose is kept. Settings → Lyrics has a **Clear lyrics cache** button.
- **YouTube Music sign-in:** fixed home recommendations for accounts whose YouTube identity differs from their Google one (for example old Google+ accounts). New **Import cookie manually** option under Settings → Account, with steps, for when the embedded sign-in picks the wrong account.
- **Playback:** the app no longer freezes when a track switch hits a stalled mpv (a likely cause of the "stuck after ~30 minutes" report). A stall now writes thread stacks to the log.
- **Fixed** Apple Music lyrics lookups failing with a `NoSuchMethodError`.
- **Windows:** rounded window corners, maximize now stays above the taskbar and un-maximizes with one click after dragging, a splash screen on the very first start, and the Matugen option is greyed out (Linux only).
